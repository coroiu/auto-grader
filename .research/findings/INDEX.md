This directory contains individual research findings. Each file documents research on a specific topic or question.

## How to Add New Findings

1. **Create a new file**: `YYYY-MM-DD-research-topic.md`
2. **Use the template** in CLAUDE.md
3. **Update this INDEX.md** with a new row
4. **Add sources to** `../references.md` if needed

## Status Meanings

- **In Progress**: Research ongoing
- **Complete**: Research concluded with findings documented
- **Needs Follow-up**: Initial research done, but more investigation needed

## Tips

- One research topic per file
- Include concrete examples and evidence
- Link to decisions that were informed by this research
- It's okay to document negative results
